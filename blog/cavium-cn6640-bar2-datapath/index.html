Reverse engineering the Cavium CN6640 BAR2 datapath

21 September 2026

The Cavium CN6640 SNIC10E is an OCTEON II based smart NIC: two 10GbE ports in front of a MIPS64 SoC that normally runs vendor firmware. That firmware is behind an NDA, and the register-level documentation is not public. This post documents how I got the card working as two ordinary 10GbE interfaces under mainline Linux, driven entirely through a reverse-engineered PCIe BAR2 datapath, with no vendor blob on the card.
